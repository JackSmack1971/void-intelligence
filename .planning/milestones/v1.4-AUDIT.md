## 3. Tech Debt & Deferred Gaps
- **Chroma Volume Management**: Currently, the vector store grows indefinitely. Future phases should implement a TTL or relevance-based pruning for the vector index.
- **SW Versioning**: The Service Worker `void-cache-v1` is static. Needs an automated versioning strategy for production CI/CD.
- **Large Graph Performance**: While IndexedDB is fast, very large graphs (>5000 nodes) may need virtualization in the `DebateGraph` visualization.
